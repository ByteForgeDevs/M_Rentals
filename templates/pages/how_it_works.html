How it works

Two questions, answered before you spend fare

Is this house real and where exactly is it, and can I trust this person. Everything below exists to answer those two questions.

  1. {% icon "shield-check" "h-5 w-5" %}

    Step 1

    Landlords get verified before they can publish

    A landlord or agent submits a national ID, plus one of: title deed, sale agreement, property management letter, agency licence, or a scheduled video call. Our team reviews every submission by hand. Until that is approved, a listing cannot go live.

  2. {% icon "camera" "h-5 w-5" %}

    Step 2

    Every listing carries a full photo set

    A listing cannot be published until it has all five required photos:

    • {% icon "check" "h-4 w-4 text-brand-600" %}Building exterior
    • {% icon "check" "h-4 w-4 text-brand-600" %}Gate or entrance
    • {% icon "check" "h-4 w-4 text-brand-600" %}Room interior
    • {% icon "check" "h-4 w-4 text-brand-600" %}Water or electricity meter
    • {% icon "check" "h-4 w-4 text-brand-600" %}Nearest landmark

    A walkthrough video is optional but pushes a listing higher in search.

  3. {% icon "map-pin" "h-5 w-5" %}

    Step 3

    Location is written the way people navigate

    Instead of trusting a map pin that may not exist for a new building, every listing names the nearest well known landmark, the walk time from it, and written directions.

    200m from Quickmart Ruaka, take the murram road opposite the petrol station, blue gate, third building past the mosque.
  4. {% icon "star" "h-5 w-5" %}

    Step 4

    Reviews are tied to a real tenancy

    When someone moves in, the landlord records the tenancy against that tenant's account. That single step unlocks both reviews: the tenant rates the landlord on responsiveness, deposit fairness and whether the house matched the listing; the landlord rates the tenant on payment, care of the property and communication. No confirmed tenancy, no review, which is what keeps the ratings worth reading.

Ready to look at real houses?

Every listing you see has passed all four steps above.
